Agentic Commerce Market Size: What the 2026 Forecasts Actually Say

2026-11-14

How big is agentic commerce? The honest answer: the forecasts agree it is large and growing quickly, but they measure different things and should not be quoted interchangeably. This article collects the major 2026 public forecasts, explains what each covers, and gives you a defensible set of numbers to use.

Why the numbers disagree

Every analyst sizes a slightly different market. Some count only agent-initiated consumer purchases. Others include business-to-business procurement, agent-driven subscriptions, and API-mediated machine payments. Some forecast worldwide figures; others cover only US retail. Before comparing numbers, check what is in the denominator.

  • McKinsey is the largest figure because it is the widest scope (global, all goods). It explicitly excludes services and most B2B, so the true total including those would be larger.

  • PayPal ($1.7T) surveyed merchants about expectations, which tends to run optimistic; treat it as sentiment, not measurement.

Current adoption signals (2026)

Separate from forward forecasts, current signals show the base is real:

  • Forrester: 11% of US online transactions over $75 already involve an AI agent.
  • Shopify: AI-driven traffic to its stores tripled year over year in Q2 2026.
  • Morgan Stanley: 23% of US adults have already tried an agentic shopping assistant.
  • x402 (Coinbase): 165 million agent transactions processed by mid-2026; Visa, Mastercard, and Stripe joined the foundation.

These are the numbers that indicate adoption is happening now, not just in 2030.

How to use these numbers responsibly

  1. Always cite the source and scope. "The US agentic commerce market could reach $300–500B by 2030 (Bain)" is defensible; "agentic commerce will be a $3.5 trillion market" without a source is not.
  1. Prefer the US e-commerce pair for US conversations. Bain and Morgan Stanley are the closest apples-to-apples comparison.
  1. Use current adoption numbers for present-tense claims. "11% of US transactions over $75 involve an AI agent (Forrester)" is a current fact; the trillion-dollar figures are forecasts, not facts.
  1. Do not add forecasts. McKinsey's $3–5T and PayPal's $1.7T are separate baskets; adding them double-counts.

What the forecasts mean for builders

All major forecasts agree on direction: agent-mediated transactions are growing from a small base into a significant share of commerce within five years. For developers and merchants, the practical implications are:

  • Be discoverable now. If your catalog is not machine-readable, you are invisible to the agent traffic that already exists (Shopify's 3× YoY signal).
  • Design for authorization. 10–25% of US e-commerce in agent hands means millions of authorization decisions per day: scoped mandates, expiring grants, auditable delegation.
  • Design for recovery. More agent transactions means more agent failures. Durable commitment records and recovery paths are the operational layer that keeps agent commerce from breaking at scale.
